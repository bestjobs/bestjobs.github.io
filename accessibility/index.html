Digital Accessibility Statement

Institutional Commitment to Full Digital Inclusivity, Non-Discrimination, and Assistive Technology Interoperability across all BestJobs Bulgaria Wings.

"MOBIKOM BULGARIA" EOOD is dedicated to ensuring equal, unencumbered access to employment notices, commercial showrooms, and economic intelligence for all citizens, including persons with disabilities, elderly individuals, and users of assistive technologies.

1. Statutory Compliance Status

This portal strictly conforms to the harmonized European Standard EN 301 549 (v3.2.1) and the international Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. Our static, zero-cookie infrastructure achieves full compliance without deploying invasive third-party overlay widgets.

2. Core Architectural Accessibility Principles

  • Perceivability: Universal 4-way view selection (Cards, Rows, Accordion, Bulletin). Native text-to-speech audio streaming. High-contrast system support (@media (forced-colors: active) and @media (prefers-contrast: more)).
  • Operability: 100% keyboard-only navigation without mouse traps. Target touch areas maintain a strict minimum dimension of 44×44 pixels. Distinctive 2-pixel focus indicators (:focus-visible).
  • Understandability: Deterministic navigation with explicit, unabbreviated terminology. Single-layer semantic HTML5 structure with unambiguous link descriptions. Zero unpredictable context shifts or flashing banners.
  • Robustness: Valid WHATWG HTML5 markup compatible with all screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, Apple VoiceOver, Android TalkBack). ARIA live region announcements for dynamic filtering and view toggles.

3. Assistive Technical Specifications

  • Markup Standard: Semantic WHATWG HTML5 with WAI-ARIA 1.2 authoring practices.
  • Styling Engine: Pure CSS Grid Level 2 with subgrid and native light-dark() Color Module 4.
  • Audio Engine: Native browser SpeechSynthesis API executing transiently in client-side volatile RAM. Zero remote telemetry.
  • Epilepsy & Vestibular Protection: Strict enforcement of @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) to neutralize transitions and layout animations.

4. Statutory Feedback & Barrier Notification Channel

Under Article 7 of Directive (EU) 2019/882, any user encountering an accessibility barrier or requiring an accessible alternate format may submit a formal notification. In accordance with our written email-only policy, reports must be directed to our administrative desk:

Open the Contact Operations Page → and select the Central Portal Desk with subject header Accessibility Barrier Report. Our compliance officers guarantee a formal response within 48 business hours.

5. Statutory Enforcement Procedure

If a satisfactory resolution is not provided within statutory deadlines, citizens may escalate complaints to the competent national market surveillance and equality bodies in the Republic of Bulgaria:

  • Ministry of e-Government (MEGOV): Directorate for Digital Accessibility and Public Information Infrastructure.
  • Commission for Protection against Discrimination (CPD): Statutory body monitoring equality and disability non-discrimination under the Anti-Discrimination Act.
