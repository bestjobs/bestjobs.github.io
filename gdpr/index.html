EUROPEAN DATA PROTECTION CORPUS • REGULATION (EU) 2016/679

Statutory GDPR Compliance & Zero-Telemetry Audit

Formal Technical Attestation of Data Protection by Design and by Default (Article 25) Across the BestJobs Network.

Statutory Privacy-by-Architecture Verification

  • Data Minimization (Article 5(1)(c)): Complete — The system collects, parses, and retains zero client personal identifiers.
  • Storage Limitation (Article 5(1)(e)): Absolute — 0 bytes of candidate credentials, identity documents, or tracking cookies exist on disk storage.
  • Security of Processing (Article 32): Hardened — Transport encryption strictly enforced via TLS 1.3, HSTS Preload, and cryptographic DNSSEC verification.
  • CPDP Authority Jurisdiction: Bulgaria — Supervised by the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД), Sofia.

1. Institutional Mandate & Scope

This compliance declaration formalizes the structural data protection framework implemented by BestJobs.bg under the operating governance of Mobikom Bulgaria Consortium. The platform functions as an independent, decentralized classified information board and economic directory for the Republic of Bulgaria and international trade counterparties.

2. Data Protection by Design and by Default (Article 25 GDPR)

Mainstream recruitment platforms compile centralized data lakes holding millions of candidate curriculum vitae (CVs), national identification numbers, phone numbers, and compensation histories — creating catastrophic systemic breach surfaces.

BestJobs.bg completely resolves data protection liabilities through architectural elimination:

  • Zero Database Footprint: BestJobs.bg operates without relational or NoSQL database instances storing visitor or applicant profiles.
  • Zero Client Tracking: The platform deploys no session cookies, advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, or dynamic behavioral scripts.
  • Client-Side Transient Execution: Interactive UI selections (such as benefit chip filters or language switches) evaluate strictly in volatile local memory, leaving zero residual forensic trace upon closing the active browser tab.

3. Total Absence of Candidate Profiling (Article 22 GDPR)

BestJobs.bg deploys zero automated decision-making engines, algorithmic screening filters, or candidate profiling mechanisms pursuant to Article 22 of the GDPR.

Candidate applications are executed directly between the individual's personal email client and the hiring employer's authenticated inbox through standardized mailto: protocols. Because BestJobs.bg never intercepts, inspects, or buffers application payloads, no data controller or data processor relationship is formed over submitted resumes.

4. Legal Grounds for System Transport Processing (Article 6 GDPR)

The minimal technical telemetry evaluated during an HTTP request (such as client IP address in transient memory) is processed strictly under Article 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interests) for the exclusive purpose of:

  • Mitigating volumetric Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) edge attacks.
  • Preventing automated scraping exploits and securing network transport integrity.
  • Enforcing cryptographic TLS 1.3 handshakes and DNSSEC zone authenticity.

Server connection logs exist as an ephemeral, volatile RAM buffer managed by Cloudflare Anycast edge filters, purging continuously without persistent user profile linkage.

5. Closed Consortium Boundary & Cross-Border Transfers (Chapter V GDPR)

BestJobs.bg executes zero international data transfers to non-adequate third countries or commercial surveillance advertising brokers. All outbound link equity is strictly restricted to our authenticated four-domain consortium: mobikom.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg.
