BestJobs Help Desk & Operating Standards

Authoritative Guidelines for Candidates, B2B Commercial Enterprises, Bank Settlements, and Accessibility Navigation.

We operate under strict sovereign principles: 100% email-only written communication, zero phone queues, zero third-party recruiter intermediation, and absolute privacy by design. Review the operational protocols below to interact effectively across all subprojects.

1. Candidate & Individual User Guide

BestJobs.bg is engineered to eliminate data profiling and recruitment agency mediation:

  • 1-Click Direct Email Application: Clicking "Apply with Email →" triggers your local email client with a structured, pre-filled formal letter containing the reference ID. Applications route directly to the hiring employer or ministry.
  • Zero Resume Storage (ZNZ Shield): We never store candidate CVs, identity documents, or phone numbers in server databases. You retain 100% custody over your personal data.
  • Mandatory Euro Remuneration: Every listing displays an explicit Gross monthly figure in Euro (€) with an automated client-side Net calculation. Undefined or negotiable salaries are prohibited.
  • 18 Social Benefit Filters: Filter positions instantly by statutory rights: Right to Disconnect, 4-Day Workweek, Annual Medical Exams, Shuttle Transit, and Bike Allowances.

2. Employer, B2B & Exhibitor Commercial Guide

To maintain an authentic white economy and eradicate spam, free commercial listings are abolished. Bank wire transfer serves as an automated KYC filter:

  • Standard Vacancy Listing (30 Days Active) • 30.00 EUR: Precisely 1.00 EUR per day. Full inclusion in regional and industry directories.
  • VIP Priority Placement & Network Syndication • 80.00 EUR: Pinned prominently at the top of directory streams with a golden VIP badge and automated XML syndication into partner media dobrichnews.com and dobruja.com.
  • Accredited Media PR Feature • 306.77 EUR: Exactly 600.00 BGN converted at the fixed BNB peg (1.95583). Permanent editorial PR article/interview published across accredited media syndicate outlets, contextually linked to your enterprise listing.
  • Strict Direct Banking Protocol: All settlements execute via direct bank wire transfer to the personal IBAN of owner/manager Stoyan Stoyanov, under "МОБИКОМ БЪЛГАРИЯ" ЕООД. We deploy zero credit card gateways (no Stripe/PayPal fees).
  • Immutable 30-Day Rule: Listings expire automatically on calendar day 31. In-place renewals are prohibited. Expired ads freeze permanently in the public archive to preserve SEO equity. To extend broadcast, a new listing must be submitted.

3. Written Email-Only Communication Protocol

Across all portal wings (Job Board, B2B Trade Mall, Energy, Sports, Real Estate), communication executes strictly via secure electronic mail:

  • Zero Phone Dispatch: We do not operate call centers, telephone lines, or messaging bots. This eradicates unsolicited commercial cold calling and guarantees documented accountability.
  • Zero Outbound Web Chat Tolls: No external live-chat widgets or third-party telecommunications trackers are loaded onto client devices.
  • Structured Processing: All inquiries, bank transfer receipts, and legal notices route cleanly through our designated desks detailed on the Contact Operations Page →.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • Are there free job postings? No. The financial barrier via bank wire acts as a strict counterparty verification filter against gray-economy actors and spam bots.
  • Can I pay with credit card or cash? No. Payments are accepted exclusively via official bank wire remittance to ensure 100% white-economy accounting.
  • Do you act as a recruitment agency? No. BestJobs.bg operates strictly as a decentralized classified bulletin under the Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ). We do not interview, screen, or mediate candidates.
  • Can I edit an expired posting? No. Expired postings freeze permanently as historical public records. A new static listing must be published.

5. Universal Accessibility & Interface Navigation Guide

In full compliance with Directive (EU) 2019/882 (European Accessibility Act) and WCAG 2.2 Level AA:

  • 4-Way View Controls: Toggle between 🔲 Cards (3-column grid), ☰ Rows (compact single lines), 📜 Accordion (native drawer expansion), and 📰 Bulletin (dense text feed).
  • Keyboard Navigation: Navigate interactive elements using Tab, activate links with Enter or Space, and dismiss drawers with Esc. Focus states display a high-contrast 2px ring.
  • Hands-Free Speech Synthesis: Click 🔊 Listen to Stream to initiate consecutive client-side text-to-speech reading in English or Bulgarian. Click ⏹ Stop Listening to halt.
  • Statutory Accessibility Statement: For complete compliance declarations, examine our formal Accessibility Statement →.
