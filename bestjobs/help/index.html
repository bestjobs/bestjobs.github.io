1. Candidate & Individual User Guide

Designed to eliminate algorithmic surveillance, resume hoarding, and agency exploitation:

  • Direct Application Architecture: Applications route cleanly to authenticated employer mailboxes with pre-formatted formal letters and reference IDs. Candidate records are never stored in server databases.
  • Contract Currency Transparency: Every posting mandates an explicit figure in contract currency (EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, etc.). Undefined or negotiable compensation is systematically rejected.
  • Anti-Bot Mathematical Gate: Protected by an in-browser math challenge ensuring human verification while keeping hiring authorities free of automated spam.
  • Zero Intermediary Fees: Candidates are never charged registration or application fees. Any external entity requesting money from job seekers will be barred and reported.
