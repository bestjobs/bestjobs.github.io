Institutional Employment Directives • Zero Compromise

  • Mandatory Euro Compensation: Every vacancy listing enforces an unambiguous Gross figure in Euro (€). Postings with undefined or negotiable salaries are systematically rejected.
  • 100% White Economy: We strictly bar unofficial cash transactions. Full social and healthcare accruals protect the candidate's statutory pension and safety rights.
  • Zero Candidate Tracking: No candidate resumes or personal data are stored in third-party server databases. Applications route cleanly to the employer's authenticated email.
  • Closed Network Integrity: Outbound active hyperlinks resolve strictly within the sovereign consortium: mobikom.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg.
  • Regulatory Notice (ЗНЗ Shield): BestJobs.bg operates strictly as a decentralized classified job advertising bulletin. The platform does not perform employment mediation, recruitment agency services, or candidate screening under the Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ). All employment relationships are established directly between the candidate and the hiring employer.
