About BestJobs Bulgaria

A Sovereign National Economic Infrastructure Built on Direct White-Economy Enterprise, Privacy by Design, and Zero-Cookie Integrity.

BestJobs.bg is owned and operated by "MOBIKOM BULGARIA" EOOD. We function as a decentralized national bulletin uniting classified employment, regional industrial trade, utility-scale energy infrastructure, and European legal directives.

Historical Lineage • Brand Evolution

The heritage of BestJobs Bulgaria is grounded in over two decades of digital classifieds and regional media operations:

  • Neogen Classifieds Inception: Originally developed within the Southeast European classifieds network alongside sister portals bestjobs.ro (Romania), bestjobs.rs (Serbia), and bestjobs.md (Moldova).
  • Regional Incubation via jobs.dobruja.com: Proved acute demand under Dobruja Media Group for transparent, direct regional employment publishing without intermediary recruiter commissions.
  • Mobikom Bulgaria Consolidation (August 15, 2013 – Present): Reorganized into a sovereign national economic portal with mandatory Euro (€) standards, immutable 30-day listing lifecycles, and a complete ban on gray-economy cash settlements.
  • Etymology: "Best Jobs" translates into the overarching Bulgarian standard: "Добре свършена работа" (A legitimate enterprise / A job well done).

Technological Manifesto • 512-Byte Web Principle

We completely reject the software bloat and surveillance tracking prevalent across mainstream web platforms. Our systems operate under strict physical and architectural constraints:

  • Static Decoupled HTML5: Zero runtime databases (No MySQL, PostgreSQL, Node daemons, or PHP). The platform cannot be brought down by backend query bottlenecks.
  • Single TCP Round-Trip (RFC 6928): Core transfer payloads fit within the initial 10 MSS congestion window (~14.6 KB), rendering layouts in <100ms on edge networks.
  • Zero Cookies & Anti-Forensics: Exactly 0 cookies. Ephemeral client state resides strictly in transient volatile RAM. Closing the browser tab reclaims all allocated pages, leaving zero forensic trace on physical client media.
  • Closed Network Equity: Outbound active hyperlinks resolve exclusively within the 4 verified consortium domains: mobikom.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg. Third-party domains remain plain text to prevent PageRank equity leakage.

Universal Accessibility • EAA & WCAG 2.2

In full alignment with Directive (EU) 2019/882 (European Accessibility Act) and EN 301 549, BestJobs incorporates native cognitive and physical accessibility layers:

  • 4-Way View Switcher: Switch dynamically between 3-column cards, compact single rows, collapsible accordions, and dense bulletin tickers.
  • Native Hands-Free Speech Synthesis: Client-side text-to-speech audio streaming in Bulgarian and English without third-party dependencies.
  • Motor & Low-Vision Protections: Full keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Space), 44px minimum touch targets, visible focus rings, and high-contrast support.
