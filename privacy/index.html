Statutory Privacy Policy

Privacy by Design, Mathematical Zero-Tracking Architecture, and Volatile Transient Operating Memory.

"MOBIKOM BULGARIA" EOOD enforces the strictest privacy standards in the European digital economy. We reject user surveillance, client fingerprinting, behavioral profiling, and persistent tracker cookies across all subproject wings.

1. Absolute Zero-Cookie Architecture

This portal deploys exactly 0 cookies. We emit zero Set-Cookie HTTP response headers at our edge layer. Consequently, no cookie consent banners or intrusive preference dialogs exist on this platform, fully conforming to the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR Article 5(3).

2. Transient Volatile RAM Execution (Anti-Forensics)

  • Zero Persistent Storage: Candidate interactions, UI view preferences, and filter configurations execute exclusively within the temporary volatile operating memory (RAM) of your web browser.
  • Zero Disk Footprint: Closing the browser tab prompts the client operating system to immediately reclaim allocated memory pages, leaving zero residual forensic trace on physical client drives.
  • Client-Side Processing: Text-to-speech audio synthesis and dynamic filtering execute 100% locally on your processor via standard browser APIs without contacting external analytical engines.

3. Candidate Data Custody (ZNZ Intermediation Immunity)

  • No Centralized CV Databases: We do not collect, hold, or sell job applications, resumes, or identity documents. Application actions trigger a direct client-side email to the authenticated employer.
  • No Recruitment Profiling: BestJobs.bg acts strictly as a decentralized classified job bulletin under the Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ). All data exchange occurs directly between the candidate and the hiring entity.
  • Zero Telemetry Scripts: We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, font CDNs, or external telemetry libraries. The font stack utilizes native operating system fonts (system-ui).

4. Statutory Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

Because we do not maintain accounts, tracking IDs, or persistent user profiles, we do not store identifying personal records of website visitors. For institutional B2B inquiries, invoice records, and verified bank wire documentation, data subjects retain full rights under GDPR Articles 15–22 (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Restriction).

To exercise statutory rights, submit a formal written notice to our Data Protection Officer via the Central Portal Desk on the Contact Operations Page →.
