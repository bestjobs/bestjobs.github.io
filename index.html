Institutional Principles • Sovereign White Economy

  • Zero Cash Transactions: All economic operations enforce 100% official bank wire settlement in Euro (€). Unofficial cash transactions are banned.
  • Pay-to-Play Verification: Commercial inclusion requires verified bank transfer to our institutional IBAN, guaranteeing authentic counterparty legitimacy.
  • Zero User Profiling: We deploy no tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or client telemetry. States execute transiently in volatile RAM.
  • Closed Consortium Interlinking: Outbound link equity remains strictly confined within our 4 verified properties: mobikom.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg.
