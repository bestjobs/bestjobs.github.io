Privacy Policy & Data Protection Framework

Zero-Cookie Architecture, Ephemeral RAM Execution, and Complete Telemetry Eradication across the BestJobs Network.

Verified Zero-Leak Technical Specification

  • Persistent Client Storage: 0 Bytes — The platform executes without cookies (HTTP Set-Cookie = 0), IndexedDB, or server-side session tracking.
  • Third-Party Telemetry: Zero — No Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, font CDNs, or marketing beacons are embedded across any endpoint.
  • Network Cloaking: Active — Origin IP addresses are strictly masked via Cloudflare Anycast proxies with DNSSEC verification.
  • Referrer Suppression: Complete — Enforced via edge HTTP headers (Referrer-Policy: no-referrer), eliminating browsing context leaks.

1. Data Controller Identification

BestJobs.bg operates as an autonomous classified bulletin and economic portal governed under the institutional framework of Mobikom Bulgaria Consortium. Official communications, data protection inquiries, and legal notifications are administered directly via our designated desk: office@bestjobs.bg.

2. Architectural Compliance with GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679)

Traditional web publishers deploy invasive cookie consent banners to mitigate regulatory liability for background surveillance. BestJobs.bg resolves compliance through technical elimination pursuant to Article 25 (Data Protection by Design and by Default).

Because the infrastructure physically lacks tracking mechanisms, profiling engines, or advertising identifier beacons, the statutory prerequisites for intrusive cookie dialogues under Article 6(1)(a) do not apply. Processing is governed strictly under Article 6(1)(f) (Legitimate Interests) for perimeter DDoS mitigation and server transport security.

3. Volatile Operating Memory (RAM) Execution

To prevent forensic data retention on client devices, client-side interactions (such as filter toggles or bookmark caching) reside strictly in volatile browser memory. Terminating the active browser tab prompts the operating system to purge allocated memory addresses, leaving zero persistent tracking footprint on physical disk media.

4. Elimination of Candidate Resume Storage (ZNZ Immunity)

BestJobs.bg explicitly refuses to act as an employment intermediary, recruitment agency, or resume repository under the Bulgarian Employment Promotion Act (ЗНЗ).

When a candidate activates an application link, the browser establishes a direct client-to-server mailto protocol with the employer's designated email address. No curriculum vitae (CV), identification documents, or personal credentials pass through or reside on BestJobs.bg infrastructure. The candidate retains absolute sovereignty over their submitted materials.

5. Outbound Network Boundary & External Isolation

Outbound hyperlinks from BestJobs.bg resolve exclusively within our authenticated four-domain consortium: mobikom.bg, dobrichnews.com, dobruja.com, and bestjobs.bg.

All hyperlinks leading to external endpoints or sister media properties enforce target="_blank" rel="noopener" attributes. Coupled with our edge Referrer-Policy: no-referrer header, external hosts receive zero referral path information when a user navigates away from the portal.

6. Exercise of Statutory Rights (Articles 15–22 GDPR)

Users retain statutory rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction. Because BestJobs.bg maintains no identifying databases, personal accounts, or email profiling lists, the most effective exercise of the "Right to be Forgotten" is clearing client-side browser cache. For formal inquiries regarding business listings or verification records, address our legal desk at office@bestjobs.bg.
