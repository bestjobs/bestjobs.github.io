GDPR Compliance Declaration

Institutional Data Protection Standards, Absolute Data Minimization, and Zero-Storage Architecture.

"MOBIKOM BULGARIA" EOOD operates BestJobs.bg under the supreme principles of Privacy by Design and Data Minimization mandated by Article 5 and Article 25 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation).

1. Strict Data Minimization & Zero Storage

  • No Candidate Databases: BestJobs.bg does not store, process, or maintain centralized databases containing candidate resumes, identity cards, or phone records.
  • No User Profiling: We deploy no behavioral tracking, no automated decision-making algorithms, and no persistent identifier cookies.
  • Volatile Memory Only: Ephemeral UI states execute strictly in temporary client-side volatile RAM and evaporate immediately upon tab closure.

2. Data Controller & Governance

  • Data Controller: "MOBIKOM BULGARIA" EOOD (EIK: 202697883), registered in the Republic of Bulgaria.
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO): Designated internal compliance office reachable strictly via written electronic mail.
  • Contact Channel: Open the Contact Operations Page → and select the DPO Desk with subject GDPR Official Inquiry.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (Article 6 GDPR)

  • Website Browsing: Zero personal data is processed or stored. No consent banner is required under ePrivacy Directive due to the physical absence of tracking cookies.
  • B2B Invoicing & Bank Settlements: Processing of legal entity representative data (name, company registration, IBAN) occurs strictly pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) (Contract Performance) and Article 6(1)(c) (Statutory Tax & Accounting Obligations under Bulgarian Law).

4. Supervisory Authority

Data subjects possess the statutory right to lodge a complaint with the lead supervisory authority in the Republic of Bulgaria:

Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД) • 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, Bulgaria • Web: cpdp.bg (unlinked plain text per consortium policy).
